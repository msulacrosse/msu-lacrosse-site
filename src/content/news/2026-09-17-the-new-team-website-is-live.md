Welcome to the new home of Michigan State Men's Lacrosse.

Everything you need is here: the full **schedule and results**, the **roster** with a page for every player, **season stats**, and this news section for updates from the team.

The schedule, roster and stats update automatically, so this is always the place to check for the latest. You can also [add the schedule to your phone's calendar](/schedule.ics) and it will keep itself up to date.
